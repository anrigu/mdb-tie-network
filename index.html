Method & caveats
Edges are employment spells parsed from career dossiers produced by a two-tier LLM pipeline (Gemini 3.1 Pro grounded sweep; Deep Research escalation for thin trails), from public sources only. People are pseudonymized (TTL-###) pending IRB review and human gold-labeling — the tie flags are unvalidated model output, not findings. Organizations kept when shared by ≥2 people or classified private-finance; the World Bank hub (linking everyone) is hidden by default. Org typing is keyword-based. Pilot generated 2026-08-12; every edge carries source URLs in the research archive.
